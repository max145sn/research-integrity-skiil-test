# Population and denominator rules

Before calculation, determine whether the unit is a row, weighted observation, unique entity, taxon, participant, plot, run, image, document, or coded segment.

Always test:

- zero-count and template rows;
- placeholders such as ND, unknown, test, blank, or dummy;
- aggregate count columns;
- duplicate identifiers and legitimate repeated measurements;
- full versus analyzed populations;
- available-case versus complete-case denominators;
- weighting;
- subgroup restrictions;
- observed versus simulated records;
- category normalization and mixed types.

If more than one plausible rule exists, record each result and classify the claim as specification-dependent or unresolved until the documented rule is identified.
