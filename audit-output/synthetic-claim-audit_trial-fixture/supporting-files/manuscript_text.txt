These results show that the method is reliable across all tabular classification domains. The study package includes machine-readable data, predictions, configurations, and analysis code. 1. Methods 2.1 Dataset The final analytical sample contained 524 observations. Each observation included three predictor variables and one binary target. No hyperparameter tuning was performed after inspecting test performance. 2.3 Evaluation procedure Performance was summarized across five independent runs.
