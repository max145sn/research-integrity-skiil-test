# Devil's Advocate Report

## Attack Plan
Step 1 pre-commitment, written before opening the manuscript or source data.

I will stress-test whether the paper establishes that ZNFX1 is a bona fide mitochondrial dsRNA sensor, rather than a downstream interferon-stimulated cofactor, RNA-binding antiviral effector, or amplifier of canonical RLR/MAVS signalling. I will hunt for: (1) evidence that ZNFX1 acts upstream of, and independently from, RIG-I/MDA5 rather than requiring their priming or shared ligands; (2) whether mitochondrial localisation is necessary for sensing/signalling, not merely observed by fractionation or imaging; (3) whether RNA binding is direct, sequence/structure-appropriate, and causally connected to IFN induction; (4) whether rescue/mutant/domain experiments separate RNA binding, ATPase/helicase motifs, MAVS interaction, localisation, and protein stability/expression; (5) whether knockout/knockdown phenotypes could reflect broad cell-health, infection-level, viral-replication, or ISG-feedback effects; (6) whether statistics and replicate data support the stated contrasts; and (7) whether blot/source-data presentation permits the claimed molecular interactions without overinterpretation.

Pre-committed fatal conditions: a Critical issue would be warranted if the submitted evidence lacks any direct causal bridge between ZNFX1's RNA binding and MAVS-dependent type I IFN induction; if the claimed RIG-I/MDA5 independence is contradicted by the design or data; if mitochondrial localisation is central to the title claim but never functionally tested; or if source data/statistics show a major quantitative result cannot be reproduced in the direction or significance claimed. I will demote points to observations if they are defensible scope limitations rather than defects in the core claim.
