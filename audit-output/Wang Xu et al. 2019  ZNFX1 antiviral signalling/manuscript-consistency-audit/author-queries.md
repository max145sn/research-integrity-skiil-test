Author Queries — Mitochondria-localised ZNFX1 functions as a dsRNA sensor to initiate antiviral responses through MAVS

Manuscript: 10.1038/s41556-019-0416-0 · *Nature Cell Biology* **21**, 1346–1356 (2019)

Ready to send. Each query locates a point in the manuscript or source data and asks the authors to clarify or supply material. Reasoning is given only where a query would otherwise be unanswerable.

---

- In the Fig. 7b block of `Source Data Fig 7.xlsx` (`Sheet1`, rows 10–35), a number of cells in different genotype and plasmid groups, and across the *IFNB1* and *IFIT1* blocks, carry identical fractional parts to seven decimal places; for example cell `E25` (*IFIT1*, wild-type, ZNFX1, replicate 2) and cell `F31` (*IFIT1*, *Mda5*<sup>−/−</sup>, RIG-I, replicate 2) both read 243.7718632, and cells `F14`, `E17`, `F27` and `E30` read 127.1500527, 87.1500527, 287.1500527 and 187.1500527 respectively. Similar groupings occur in the Fig. 7c block in rows 41 and 42, where four of the six non-unity values in each row agree to five leading decimal digits. Could you explain how these values were generated and supply the primary qRT–PCR output — Ct values or equivalent instrument export — from which the relative expression values in Figs. 7b and 7c were calculated, so that the relationship between them can be followed?

- In the Fig. 1h block of `Source Data Fig 1.xlsx` (`Sheet1`, rows 47–56), the second replicate of the IFN-α series and the second replicate of the IFN-β series agree to six decimal places at all four time points: 10.3744536 against 10.3744568 at 4 h, 12.4231824 against 12.4231872 at 8 h, 8.3684621 against 8.3684624 at 16 h, and 7.3104623 against 7.3104625 at 24 h, while the first and third replicates of the two series differ in the normal way. Since Fig. 1h presents IFN-α and IFN-β stimulation as separate treatments, could you clarify how the second replicate series were obtained and supply the underlying Ct values for both?

- The Fig. 7c legend describes "wild-type or *Mda5*<sup>−/−</sup> A549 cells", the corresponding sentence in the Results refers only to *Mda5*<sup>−/−</sup> cells, and the Fig. 7c block of `Source Data Fig 7.xlsx` is labelled `Rig-i -/-` (rows 38–40) and `Mda5 -/-` (rows 41–43) with no wild-type group. Could you confirm which two cell lines are plotted in Fig. 7c and let us know whether the legend and the Results sentence need to be corrected?

- In the Fig. 7c block of `Source Data Fig 7.xlsx`, the *Rig-I*<sup>−/−</sup> MDA5+VSV group (cells `F38:F40`) reads 45514.8509464, 42552.9712536 and 4176.8590572, with the third replicate roughly ten-fold below the other two. Could you check this third value against the original run and confirm or correct it?

- The Fig. 2 legend states "For **d,e,h–j**, *n* = 3 independent experiments", but in `Source Data Fig 2.xlsx` (`Sheet1`) the uninfected/VSV and uninfected/EMCV blocks of Fig. 2h contain three replicate rows (50–52) while the uninfected/H1N1 and uninfected/HSV-1 blocks contain four (57–60). Could you confirm the number of independent experiments behind each panel of Fig. 2h and tell us whether the legend should distinguish them?

- The Fig. 7d legend describes WT, *Rig-I*<sup>−/−</sup> and *Mda5*<sup>−/−</sup> cells transfected with ZNFX1, RIG-I or MDA5 plasmids or EV, and Fig. 7e is described as the statistical analysis of those experiments, but the Fig. 7e block of `Source Data Fig 7.xlsx` (rows 47–58, columns J–K) contains only `EV` and `EV+VSV-GFP` columns. The Fig. 7g block (rows 61–67, columns J–K) similarly contains only `EV` and `EV+VSV` for *IFNB1* and *IFIT1*, without the plasmid or poly(I:C) conditions that the g,h legend describes and that the adjacent Fig. 7h block does include. Could you supply the remaining source data for both panels?

- In the Fig. 7i block of `Source Data Fig 7.xlsx` (rows 96–100), the genotype header row labels four of the six columns `WT` or `Rig-i-/-` but leaves the two siZNFX1 columns blank, so those two conditions cannot be assigned to a cell line. Could you tell us which genotype the `siZNFX1` and `siZNFX1+VSV` columns correspond to?

- `Source Data Fig 4.pdf` contains uncropped blots under the headings `Figure 4 b` and `Figure 4c`, although the Fig. 4 legend describes panels b and c as luciferase reporter assays; within the same file the bands listed under `Figure 4f` (native PAGE and SDS PAGE, IRF3, GAPDH, ZNFX1) correspond to what the legend describes for panel h, those under `Figure 4h` (p-IRF3, p-TBK1, IRF3, GAPDH) to what the legend describes for panel g, and those under `Figure 4g` (RIG-I, ZNFX1-flag, TBK1, GAPDH) to what the legend describes for panel f. `Source Data Fig 5.pdf` likewise contains a blot headed `Figure 5b`, a panel the legend describes as a qRT–PCR analysis, and `Source Data Fig 6.pdf` contains one headed `Figure 6j`, also described in the legend as a qRT–PCR analysis. Could you confirm which published panel each uncropped blot belongs to and relabel the three files accordingly?

- The header row for the Fig. 1e left panel in `Source Data Fig 1.xlsx` (`Sheet1`, row 9) lists ten tissues — Muscle, Brain, Lymph node, Thymus, liver, Bowel, Heart, Kideny, Lung and Spleen — whereas the published Fig. 1e left panel appears to plot nine, without liver; the right panel of the same figure does include Liver. Could you confirm whether the liver measurement is plotted in the left panel, and correct either the panel or the source data as appropriate?

- The Data availability statement reads "Source data for Figs. 1–7 and Extended Data Figs. 1–7 have been provided as Statistics Source Data", while the Reporting Summary reads "Statistical source data for Fig. 1-7 and Supplementary Fig. 1-6 are provided in Supplementary Table". Could you reconcile the two statements? Relatedly, the files supplied for Figs. 4, 5 and 6 contain uncropped blots only, with no numeric values, although Fig. 4a–e,i, Fig. 5b,c,e,f and Fig. 6j are quantitative panels plotted as mean ± s.d. with *P* values — could you supply the numeric source data for those panels?

- In the Discussion, the claim that "the constitutive mRNA or protein levels of ZNFX1 are much higher than those of RIG-I and MDA5 in various tissues and cell types" cites Fig. 1i and Extended Data Figs. 1f, 2f and 5a–c. The Fig. 1i legend describes an immunoblot of cells treated with IFN-α or IFN-β, the Extended Data Fig. 1f legend describes RNA levels after viral infection, and the Extended Data Fig. 2f legend describes immunoblot confirmation of target-gene deficiency in CRISPR-Cas9 knockout clones. Could you confirm which of these references is intended to support the constitutive-expression comparison and adjust the citation if appropriate?

- The Methods section "Flow cytometry" states that for gene knockdown, A549 and L929 cells were "challenged with VSV-eGFP for 6 h at 2 MOI", while the Fig. 2a block of `Source Data Fig 2.xlsx` carries four condition labels: `2 MOI (6h)` and `0.5 MOI (12h)` for A549, and `5 MOI (6h)` and `1 MOI (12h)` for L929. Could you extend the Methods to cover the conditions actually used?

- The Fig. 2g block of `Source Data Fig 2.xlsx` (rows 45 and 46) contains two separate HSV-1 rows, labelled `HSV-1 low` and `HSV-1 high`, but the Fig. 2g legend and the corresponding sentence in the Results refer to HSV-1 without distinguishing a dose. Could you confirm whether both doses are plotted and, if so, specify them in the legend and in the sentence reporting the HSV-1 result?

- The Author contributions statement ends "S.Y. and X.J. are joint co first authors", but S.Y. and X.J. are the second and third authors in the list, the first author Y.W. is not included in the statement, and the title page does not carry a corresponding equal-contribution footnote. Could you confirm which authors are intended as joint first authors so that the title page and the statement can be brought into line?

- In the Results paragraph beginning "ZNFX1-mediated signalling is independent of RIG-I and MDA5", the statement that endogenous co-immunoprecipitation showed ZNFX1 interacts with neither RIG-I nor MDA5 in resting or VSV-infected cells is not accompanied by a figure citation, unlike the other results in that paragraph. Could you add the relevant figure reference, or indicate if the experiment is not shown?

- A few spellings differ between locations or appear to be errors: the Fig. 1e axis and the header cell `H9` of `Source Data Fig 1.xlsx` read "Kideny", while the Fig. 1e right-panel source-data block spells the same tissue "kidney"; the Fig. 1l legend reads "ΔZnfx1 promotor-Luc" while the corresponding source-data rows read "△Znfx1 promoter-Luc"; the Fig. 5e legend reads "includeing of five coding genes"; the Results refer to "Senda" virus where the Fig. 5c legend uses "SeV"; and the Discussion contains "ZFNX1". Could you confirm the intended spelling in each case?

- In the RIG-I HIV-treated column of the Fig. 1f block in `Source Data Fig 1.xlsx` (`Sheet1`, rows 34, 38 and 41), the value 0.249075 appears three times among the fourteen values. We understand Fig. 1f to be a re-analysis of public GEO microarray datasets, in which repeated processed values can arise legitimately; could you confirm that these three entries are the values as retrieved, and identify which accession they come from?
