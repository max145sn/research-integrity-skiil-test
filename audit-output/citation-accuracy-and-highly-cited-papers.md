# Citation Accuracy Check & Highly-Cited Papers in the Field

**Document audited:** Ratner, Burrow & Thoemmes (2016), *R. Soc. Open Sci.* 3:160431 — "The effects of exposure to objective coherence on perceived meaning in life: a preregistered direct replication of Heintzelman, Trent & King (2013)."
**Method:** Every DOI-bearing reference (31 of 42) was resolved against the live CrossRef metadata record and compared to the citation as printed in the manuscript's reference list (author names, year, title). CrossRef `is-referenced-by-count` was used as a citation-count proxy (Google Scholar/Web of Science access is not available in this environment, so counts here are a conservative floor — Scholar counts typically run 1.3–2x higher).

## 3. Caveats
- Counts reflect CrossRef's indexed citation graph as of the query date and understate true citation counts (Google Scholar/Web of Science are not reachable from this environment); use them for *relative* ranking, not absolute totals.
- This check validates bibliographic **accuracy and existence**, not whether each citation correctly represents its source's findings (a full claim-to-source semantic audit, as done via `paper-claim-audit`, would be a separate/deeper pass).
